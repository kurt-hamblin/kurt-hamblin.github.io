A casual introduction to machine learning that I gave to the summer REU students at KU for several years, with slides and a Google Colab notebook exercise (about 1.5 hours). My goal was to get students with some Python experience engaged in the topic and to de-mystify it a bit.

One of the notebooks has the "answers." Feel free to use and edit the materials as you see fit.
