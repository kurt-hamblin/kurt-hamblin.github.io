## Low-luminosity AGN fitting

A fitting method that splits a galaxy's JWST photometry into star-forming and AGN components and returns a probability distribution for the AGN contribution. I wrote it for the last part of my dissertation (see [Research](/research/#low-luminosity-agn)).

## Valithria Dreamwalker healing (World of Warcraft, 2023)

A short writeup on whether a hunter's Tenacity pet meaningfully increases Holy Paladin healing on the Valithria Dreamwalker raid encounter. I worked out the effective healing per Holy Light cast analytically, then simulated a full encounter, and found the pet saves about 4 to 8 seconds per Holy Paladin. [Writeup (PDF)](/files/valithria-tenacity-pet.pdf)

## KU REU machine learning workshop

A casual introduction to machine learning that I gave to the summer REU students at KU for several years, with slides and a Google Colab notebook exercise (about 1.5 hours). My goal was to get students with some Python experience engaged in the topic and to de-mystify it a bit. One of the notebooks has the "answers." Feel free to use and edit the materials as you see fit. [Materials (Google Drive)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/112MLkjuoajzMHIh5-CcxXuxOoe374KFJ?usp=drive_link)
